*Last updated: 2026-07-07*

Bonnag is a free assistant that sends you reminders ("nudges") about goals you set, over WhatsApp. It is provided by **Kieran Elby** (see the [Privacy policy](/nudges/privacy/)). By messaging Bonnag you agree to these terms.

**Bonnag is a reminder tool, nothing more.** It does not provide medical, health, mental-health, nutritional, financial, legal, or any other professional advice, and nothing it says should be treated as such. Any calorie figures or similar are rough estimates for your own reference only. Always consult a qualified professional for advice about your health or wellbeing.

## 3. Not for emergencies

Bonnag is **not** a crisis or emergency service and cannot help in an emergency. If you are in danger or need urgent help, contact your local emergency services. In the UK, the Samaritans are available free, 24/7, on **116 123**. If Bonnag detects that a request is beyond what it can safely help with, it will say so, point you to appropriate resources, and stop sending nudges.

## 4. Who can use it

You must be **18 or over** to use Bonnag. Please don't use it on behalf of anyone else, and don't rely on it for anything important or safety-critical.
