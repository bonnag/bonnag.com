# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-07-07*

## Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for the Bonnag nudge assistant is **Kieran Elby**.

Contact: **[nudges@bonnag.com](mailto:nudges@bonnag.com)**.

## What this covers

This policy covers the **Bonnag WhatsApp nudge assistant** — the bot you message to receive reminders about your goals. It does not cover the rest of bonnag.com.

## What we collect

We only hold what you send us and what the service needs to work:

- **Your messaging identifier** — the identifier WhatsApp gives us for your account (a phone-number JID or a privacy-preserving linked identifier). We treat this as an opaque key; we do not separately store your phone number.
- **What you tell Bonnag** — the name you'd like to be called, the goals you're working towards, how often you want nudges and when, any reminders, and any progress you choose to log.
- **Your conversation** — both sides of your messages with Bonnag, kept in raw form.
- **Operational records** — timestamps, message counts (for rate-limiting and abuse prevention), and, where relevant, a record that a message was flagged for safety review.

We **do not** open, store, or process images, videos, or links you send, and we do not follow links.

## Why we hold it, and our legal basis

We use your information solely to provide the nudge service — to understand your requests, send your nudges and reminders, remember your progress, and keep the service safe and within messaging limits.

Legal basis: your consent (given by choosing to use the service) and/or our legitimate interest in providing a service you asked for.

If you ask Bonnag to track sensitive personal data (such as health-related information, including weight), we will confirm with you that we have your explicit consent to store it.

## Where your data is held, and who processes it

Your data is stored on a server in **London, United Kingdom** (hosted by OVH).

To deliver the service we rely on these third-party processors:

- **WhatsApp / Meta** — delivers messages between you and Bonnag. Your use of WhatsApp is also governed by their own terms and privacy policy.
- **Anthropic** — the AI provider whose model reads your messages to understand them and helps draft Bonnag's replies. Anthropic processes data in the **United States**, so using Bonnag involves an international transfer of your messages. See below.
- **OVH** — hosts the server (London, UK).

We never share your information with other Bonnag users, and we don't sell it or use it for advertising.

## How we transfer your data outside the UK

Because Anthropic's servers are located in the United States, your personal data is transferred outside the UK when we use this tool. To keep your information safe and comply with the UK GDPR, we do the following:

- **Paid commercial security** — we only use Anthropic's paid business platforms. This guarantees that your prompt data is strictly confidential and is never used to train the AI model.
- **Legal protections (UK Addendum)** — our agreement with Anthropic automatically includes the UK Addendum to the Standard Contractual Clauses. This is a legally binding contract approved by the UK Government to protect data sent to the US.
- **Risk assessments** — we have conducted a Transfer Risk Assessment (TRA) to ensure that the data we process is low-risk and handled securely.
- **Data minimisation** — we actively strip out or anonymise highly sensitive or unnecessary personal identifiers before inputting data into the AI.

## How long we keep it

We keep conversation history and related records for a documented period of six years, after which it is deleted. If you ask to be forgotten (see below), we delete your information sooner — see the exception under "Deletion".

## Your rights

You can, at any time, simply by messaging Bonnag:

- **Get a copy of your data** — message **"export my data"** and Bonnag sends you everything it holds about you.
- **Be forgotten** — message **"forget me"**; after you confirm, Bonnag erases your information and conversation history.

You also have rights under UK data protection law to access, correct, or restrict the use of your data, and to complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk). To exercise any right, or if something isn't working through the bot, email **[nudges@bonnag.com](mailto:nudges@bonnag.com)**.

### Deletion

When you ask to be forgotten we delete your profile, goals, progress, reminders, and message history. We retain only a minimal record **that** a deletion was requested, so we can demonstrate we honoured it. Some copies may persist briefly in routine backups before they age out.

## Safety

If your messages suggest you may be at risk (for example, self-harm or a crisis), Bonnag will stop sending nudges, point you to real sources of help, and flag the exchange for a person on the team to review. This is to keep you safe; the relevant messages are handled accordingly.

## Security

Messages between WhatsApp and Bonnag are transmitted over encrypted connections, and inbound requests to Bonnag are authenticated. We also keep all personal data encrypted at rest. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information. Upon receiving any notification of a security incident, we will immediately isolate the affected data, assess the impact, and — where required by the UK GDPR — notify the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) and any affected individuals within 72 hours.

## This website

bonnag.com is served via GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, which may set cookies or log request metadata as part of delivering and protecting the site. The nudge assistant itself runs over WhatsApp, not this website.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date. Significant changes will be communicated to existing users via WhatsApp.
