# Using Bonnag

You talk to Bonnag in plain English — there are no commands to learn. Here are the things people find useful.

## Getting set up

- **Name** — "call me Sam" (or "call me Sammy instead").
- **A goal** — "I want to go to the gym more", "trying to drink more water", "no snacking". You can have **more than one** goal at a time.
- **How often** — "nudge me twice a week", "once a day is fine".

## Changing how often it messages you

- "these are coming too often" / "drop to twice a week"
- "not before 9am please" · "nothing at night" · "mornings only"
- "you can nudge me about the gym again" — turn nudges for one goal back on

## Reminders

For a specific thing at a specific time:

- "remind me about the dentist on Thursday at 2"
- "what reminders do I have?" · "cancel the dentist one"

## Logging progress (optional)

- "went to the gym yesterday" · "did 20 press-ups" · "1.5L of water today"
- "how am I doing?" · "what's my total this week?"

If you mention a food, Bonnag will note an approximate calorie figure — its own rough estimate unless you give one. You can also just ask: "how many calories in a banana?"

## Taking a break

- **"pause"** — Bonnag stops messaging until you say otherwise. (Reminders you've specifically asked for still arrive.)
- "ok back" — pick up where you left off.

## Your data

- **"export my data"** — Bonnag sends you a copy of everything it holds about you.
- **"forget me"** — it deletes your information and history (it asks you to confirm first).

## Talking to a person

If you'd like a human from the team to look at something, say **"talk to someone"** and your request is logged for someone to pick up.

## Good to know

- Bonnag won't coach you or give tips, techniques, or advice on *how* to reach a goal — it remembers and reminds, that's the job.
- It won't comment on your health or give medical advice.
- A quick "thanks" or "cool" doesn't always get a reply — that's deliberate, not a fault.
- It only understands English, and it doesn't open images, videos, or links.
