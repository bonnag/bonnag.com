# Bonnag

Bonnag is a little assistant that helps you chip away at your goals by sending small reminders — *nudges* — over WhatsApp.

It's not a coach, a therapist, or a medical service. It doesn't tell you *how* to do things, and it won't lecture you. It just remembers what you're working towards and gives you a gentle prod now and then — at a frequency you choose.

## How it works

1. **Message Bonnag on WhatsApp.** *(The link to start is coming soon.)*
2. **Tell it what you're working towards** — roughly is fine ("go to the gym more", "drink more water", "no snacking").
3. **Say how often you'd like a nudge** — a few times a week, once a day, whatever suits.

That's it. Bonnag starts sending short, friendly nudges. You can change any of it at any time, just by telling it.

## What Bonnag does

- Sends brief nudges toward the goals you've set.
- Remembers what you've told it and what you've logged.
- Lets you set one-off reminders ("remind me about the dentist on Thursday").
- Tracks simple progress if you want ("did 20 press-ups", "1.5L of water today").
- Backs off whenever you ask — just say **"pause"**.

## What Bonnag won't do

- It won't coach you, give tips or techniques, or tell you the "right" way to reach a goal.
- It won't give medical, health, or mental-health advice, or make diagnoses.
- It won't guilt-trip you, pressure you, or shame you for a missed day.
- It won't read images, videos, or links you send, and it only speaks English.

## Your data, your control

You're in charge of what Bonnag holds about you. At any time you can message it to:

- **Export** everything it knows about you, or
- **Forget** you — it deletes your information and history.

See the [Privacy policy](/nudges/privacy/) for the detail.

## If you're really struggling

Bonnag is just a goal-reminder bot — it isn't built to help with a crisis, and it will say so honestly if something you write is beyond what it can safely help with. If you need to talk to someone now:

- **Samaritans** (UK) — call **116 123**, free, 24/7.
- Elsewhere — [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) lists local options.
